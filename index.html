FAQs – Frequently Asked Questions

The festival takes place in Frankfurt City!

Mainhaus Stadthotel Frankfurt City Center, Rothschildallee 16A, 60389 Frankfurt am Main

The Frankfurt Bachata Festival takes place from 22nd to 25th April 2027.

All prices are listed in the Tickets section. Early Bird prices apply to the first 50 tickets only.

During a Bachata Festival there are socials all day and night, workshops, shows, competitions and much more. It is a 4-day celebration of passion, rhythm, and pure joy!

Yes, there are several accommodation options available in close proximity to the festival venue. We recommend booking early to secure your preferred choice.

The Fullpass Ticket grants you access to all festival activities except the special bootcamps, including workshops, performances, parties, and the live concert.

The Partypass Ticket grants you access to all festival parties and the live concert.

There will be day pass tickets. They grant you access to all festival activities during one day, except the special bootcamps.

There is no specific dress code during the day, but for the parties we will have amazing mottos, so make sure you dress up.

Absolutely! Anyone can participate. Just register & showcase your Bachata skills.

You can change the name on your ticket for a small fee of 15 EUR or get a ticket for the next event.

Yes, there is parking available near the festival venue. You can park on the streets, in a parking garage or in front of the REWE supermarket close to the venue.
